# 08 — Ecom Sample Request Training Guide

**Role:** Cross-team enablement and intake clarity document  
**Audience:** Buying team, Buyer's Assistants, cross-functional partners  
**Status:** Active

---

## 1. Purpose of This Guide

This guide is designed to help Buyer's Assistants (BAs) understand how to read and properly navigate the shared Ecom Sample Request spreadsheet. It ensures that everyone follows the same process flow, uses the spreadsheet consistently, and provides Ecom with the information needed to move samples through the description and photography process efficiently.

---

## 2. The Content Specialist's Role (Simplified)

As the Content Specialist, I am responsible for:

- **Creating Product Descriptions:** Using the samples you provide, I write the product descriptions that appear online.
- **Managing Sample Flow Through Ecom:** Once you drop samples at Ecom, I make sure they move through the right steps:
  - IG checks them in and sorts by priority.
  - I write descriptions.
  - Samples go to Photography for images.
- **Keeping the Process Organized:** I mark when samples are received by Ecom and when they're turned into Photography. At the end of each week, I check if photos are complete and remove those items from your tab.

**Why this matters to you:**
- Once you hand samples off to Ecom (via IG), your job is complete.
- You don't need to track what happens afterward — I manage the rest of the process.
- The clearer your notes and sample tracking are, the faster we can get products described, shot, online, and back to you.

---

## 3. What Ecom Needs From the Spreadsheet

At a minimum, every line item in your tab should clearly show:

1. Ecom Color Number
2. Ecom Style Number
3. Current Status / Location of the Sample (e.g., requested, dropped off to Ecom, with another dept)

---

## 4. Notes Section Best Practices

✅ **Good Notes (Simple, Clear):**
- Requested from vendor
- Requested from DC
- Dropped at Ecom — IG
- Returned to store per [dept] request

❌ **Bad Notes (Confusing, Too Much Detail):**
- Vendor says maybe next week if truck comes early but not sure, checking again soon.
- This one's tricky — might need to ask around.

---

## 5. Color-Coding in the Spreadsheet

- **Orange/Brown** = Sample received by Ecom
- **Green** = Sample turned in to Photography

*(BAs do not need to assign these colors to entire rows — Ecom will update them.)*

---

## 6. Where You Have Flexibility

- You can manage your tab in the way that keeps you most organized, as long as the style number, color number, and sample status are always up to date.
- You can use notes to keep the flow moving, but keep them simple and consistent so anyone can understand.

---

## 7. Weekly Clean-Up

At the end of each week, Ecom removes completed items from your tab. This keeps your list clean and focused only on active samples.

---

## 8. Questions & Feedback

If anything in this process feels unclear or slows you down, reach out directly. Your feedback helps refine the process for everyone.
