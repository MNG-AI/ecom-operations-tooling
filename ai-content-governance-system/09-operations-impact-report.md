# 09 — eCommerce Operations Impact Report

**Period:** May 2025 – Nov. 2025  
**Role:** Performance reporting — documents operational outcomes tied to this system  
**Audience:** Leadership, performance reviews, portfolio use

---

## Key Performance Highlights

- **1,600+ products tracked, received, and released** through the Ecom sample workflow.
- **Average 3–5-day turnaround** from sample receipt to photography — reduced from **15 days in August** (Back-to-School peak).
- **Sample tracking system added (June 2025)** → visibility and faster prioritization.
- **Authored three internal content frameworks** — *The Ecom Way, The SS Way, The SC Way*
- **Python QA tool** built to locate broken or misdirected brand-page links.
- **Cross-team collaboration** across Buying, Photography, Marketing, and Customer Care to ensure product accuracy and launch readiness.

---

## Process Optimization & Quality Control

- Added **date-tracking and pivot analysis** to monitor workflow speed.
- Simplified tab structure in the master sample sheet for clearer ownership.
- Proposed **"Done File" archive** for completed SKUs to prevent duplication.
- Deployed **Python QA script** to catch brand-link errors before launch.
- Audited product details, inventory, and sizing based on feedback or service tickets.

---

## Content Framework Development

Created standardized copywriting systems for both banners:

- **The Ecom Way:** structure for all product descriptions.
- **The SS Way:** premium, lifestyle tone for brand page copy.
- **The SC Way:** energetic, family-value tone for brand page copy.

Wrote and optimized brand page copy for key brands across both banners.

---

## Cross-Team Collaboration

- Coordinated weekly with **Buying, Photography, and Marketing** to keep sample movement on schedule.
- Partnered with **Customer Care** to fix product details flagged by customers.
- Maintained continuous communication flow across departments for product accuracy.

---

## Insights & Next Steps

- Expand tracking to include **Photography → Site Live** phase for full visibility.
- Build **automated dashboard** summarizing backlog and turnaround.
- Connect **GA4 metrics** (time-on-page, bounce rate) to content completeness.
- Create a company-wide **framework** for all copy across both banners.
