# 01 — AI-Assisted Content Governance & Enablement: System Overview

**Role:** System overview — non-technical  
**Audience:** Leadership, stakeholders, future owners  
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose

This document outlines the systems already in use to support consistent AI-assisted content within eCommerce content workflows. The goal is to prevent unverified or inconsistent content from reaching the site while keeping existing processes practical.

---

## Why This Exists

- AI is being used more often to support content creation.
- Without clear checks, AI can introduce inaccuracies, inconsistent language, or extra QA work downstream.
- These tools and guidelines were built to help teams use AI without creating unfamiliar problems in production.

---

## What Is Currently in Place

The following tools and processes are already being used within eCommerce content work:

- AI-assisted description writing that follows existing brand, SEO, and structure standards.
- Feature verification and simplification to ensure customer-friendly language stays correct.
- Approved feature lists to prevent unapproved terms or claims from being used.
- Clear stop rules when product information cannot be verified.
- QA checks to confirm structure, accuracy, and eligibility before content goes live.
- Training and process guides so non-technical users can work consistently.

> *Together, these function as a set of guardrails rather than a single tool or prompt.*

---

## Where This Is Already Working

- Product description creation and rewrites
- Brand page copy
- Sample-to-site eCommerce workflows

*The operational impact of these systems has already been tracked through existing reports.*

---

## Problems This Helps Prevent

- Incorrect or unverifiable AI-assisted content going live.
- Inconsistent tone or structure across similar content.
- Knowledge being locked to specific tools or individuals.
- Extra QA work caused by fixing issues late in the process.

---

## Current Scope

eCommerce Content Operations

## Ownership

Developed within eCommerce content workflows to support accuracy, efficiency, and consistency.
